# Scope of the additional archived snapshot

`preserved_artifacts/additional_model_archives/` and `preserved_reports/additional_model_archives/` contain surviving material from a second archived repository snapshot organized by Claude model.

This material is included to expose additional preserved experimental evidence that is already available to the research team. The second snapshot contains more experiment/node identifiers than the principal Branch 1/Branch 2 archive and may include auxiliary or development executions in addition to runs corresponding to the reported evaluation. The package therefore **does not treat every directory in the additional snapshot as a canonical manuscript result** and does not infer a mapping that is not explicitly stored in the archive.

The quantitative results reported in the manuscript should be interpreted from the manuscript's consolidated results and associated recorded tracking evidence. The additional snapshot is provided for transparency and inspection of the surviving generated artifacts, metric reports, and runtime evidence.

The original model-oriented directory names are retained to preserve provenance. No attempt is made to rename these directories into Branch 1/Branch 2 categories when the archived material itself does not explicitly establish that mapping.
